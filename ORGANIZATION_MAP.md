## 📋 Organization Principles

1. **NEVER REDUCE** - All files preserved
2. **ALWAYS ENHANCE** - Improved structure
3. **Logical Grouping** - Related items together
4. **Priority Numbering** - Important items first
5. **Type Separation** - Archives, docs, media separate
6. **Quick Discovery** - Easy to find anything
7. **Scalable Structure** - Easy to add new items
